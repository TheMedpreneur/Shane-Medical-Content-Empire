# Video Templates

Pre-configured video templates for medical education content production.

## Template Categories

### Lecture Templates
- Standard lecture format
- Picture-in-picture presenter
- Split-screen with slides
- Virtual classroom environment

### Procedure Templates
- Surgical procedure documentation
- Clinical skills demonstration
- Laboratory techniques
- Patient examination procedures

### Interactive Templates
- Quiz integration overlays
- Annotation and markup tools
- 3D model rotation displays
- Multi-angle procedure views

### Branding Templates
- Intro sequences
- Outro and credits
- Lower thirds
- Transition effects
- Logo animations

## Template Specifications

### Video Settings
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (1080p) minimum, 3840x2160 (4K) recommended
- **Frame Rate**: 30 fps standard, 60 fps for procedural content
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 standard
- **Format**: MP4 (H.264/H.265)

### Audio Settings
- **Sample Rate**: 48 kHz
- **Bit Depth**: 24-bit
- **Channels**: Stereo or mono for narration
- **Format**: AAC

## Usage Instructions

1. Select appropriate template for content type
2. Import template into video editing software
3. Replace placeholder content with actual material
4. Customize branding elements
5. Export with specified settings
6. Quality check before publication

## Customization

Templates are fully customizable:
- Colors and branding
- Typography and text styles
- Transitions and effects
- Timing and duration
- Audio levels and mixing

## Accessibility

All templates include:
- Closed caption support
- High contrast options
- Clear typography
- Descriptive audio tracks
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance

## Software Compatibility

Templates available for:
- Adobe Premiere Pro
- Adobe After Effects
- DaVinci Resolve
- Final Cut Pro
- Unreal Engine Sequencer

## Best Practices

- Maintain consistent branding
- Use clear, readable fonts
- Ensure adequate contrast
- Keep animations professional
- Test on multiple devices
- Consider accessibility needs
