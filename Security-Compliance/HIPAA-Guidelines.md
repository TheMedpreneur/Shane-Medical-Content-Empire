# HIPAA Compliance Guidelines

## Overview

This document outlines HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) compliance requirements for the medical education content system.

## Protected Health Information (PHI)

### What is PHI?
PHI includes any individually identifiable health information that is:
- Transmitted or maintained in any form (electronic, paper, oral)
- Created or received by a healthcare provider, health plan, or healthcare clearinghouse
- Related to past, present, or future physical/mental health, healthcare provision, or payment

### 18 HIPAA Identifiers
Never include any of the following in medical education content:
1. Names
2. Geographic subdivisions smaller than state
3. Dates (except year) related to individuals
4. Telephone numbers
5. Fax numbers
6. Email addresses
7. Social Security numbers
8. Medical record numbers
9. Health plan beneficiary numbers
10. Account numbers
11. Certificate/license numbers
12. Vehicle identifiers and serial numbers
13. Device identifiers and serial numbers
14. URLs
15. IP addresses
16. Biometric identifiers
17. Full-face photos and comparable images
18. Any other unique identifying characteristic or code

## De-identification Requirements

### Safe Harbor Method
Remove all 18 identifiers listed above, and ensure no remaining information could be used to identify an individual.

### Expert Determination Method
A qualified expert determines that the risk is very small that the information could be used to identify an individual.

## Medical Education Content Guidelines

### Acceptable Content
✅ Generalized medical scenarios
✅ Composite cases (fictional combinations)
✅ Standardized patient cases
✅ Anatomical models and diagrams
✅ De-identified imaging studies
✅ Statistical and aggregate data

### Prohibited Content
❌ Real patient photos without consent
❌ Actual patient medical records
❌ Identifiable patient information
❌ Real patient stories without authorization
❌ Video/audio with identifiable patients

## Security Requirements

### Technical Safeguards
- **Access Controls**: Unique user IDs, emergency access procedures
- **Audit Controls**: Record and examine system activity
- **Integrity Controls**: Protect against improper alteration/destruction
- **Transmission Security**: Encrypt PHI during transmission

### Physical Safeguards
- **Facility Access Controls**: Limit physical access to systems
- **Workstation Security**: Policies for workstation use
- **Device Controls**: Control movement of hardware and software

### Administrative Safeguards
- **Security Management**: Risk analysis and management
- **Workforce Training**: Security awareness and training
- **Contingency Planning**: Disaster recovery and backup

## Training Requirements

All team members must complete:
- Initial HIPAA training within 30 days of hire
- Annual refresher training
- Additional training when policies change
- Documentation of all training

## Incident Response

### If PHI is Potentially Exposed
1. **Immediate**: Stop the exposure/breach
2. **Within 1 hour**: Report to Security Officer
3. **Within 24 hours**: Document the incident
4. **Within 60 days**: Notify affected individuals (if applicable)
5. **Continuous**: Investigate and prevent future incidents

### Breach Notification Requirements
- Individual notification
- Media notification (if >500 individuals affected)
- HHS notification
- Document all breach activities

## Audit and Monitoring

### Regular Audits
- Monthly access log reviews
- Quarterly security assessments
- Annual comprehensive HIPAA audit
- Random spot checks

### Documentation
- All policies and procedures
- Training records
- Risk assessments
- Audit logs
- Incident reports

## Vendor Management

### Business Associate Agreements (BAA)
Required when vendors have access to PHI:
- Define permitted uses and disclosures
- Require appropriate safeguards
- Require breach reporting
- Include termination provisions

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

### Civil Penalties
- Tier 1 (Unknown): $100 - $50,000 per violation
- Tier 2 (Reasonable Cause): $1,000 - $50,000 per violation
- Tier 3 (Willful Neglect, Corrected): $10,000 - $50,000 per violation
- Tier 4 (Willful Neglect, Not Corrected): $50,000 per violation

### Criminal Penalties
- Up to $50,000 and 1 year in prison (unknowing violations)
- Up to $100,000 and 5 years in prison (false pretenses)
- Up to $250,000 and 10 years in prison (intent to sell/use PHI)
